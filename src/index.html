Mosque Finder Map

Find mosques near you, or in any city you search for, on an interactive map. It works in the browser with no app or sign-up.

Open the full mosque finder →

How to find a mosque

  1. Allow location access to see mosques around you, or type a city name into the search box.
  2. Pan and zoom the map. Each pin is a mosque.
  3. Tap a pin to see the mosque's name.

Where the data comes from

Mosque locations come from OpenStreetMap, a free map built by volunteers. Coverage is good in well-mapped areas and patchier elsewhere. If a mosque you know is missing, you can add it to OpenStreetMap yourself.

Travelling? Check the prayer times for the city you are in, and find the Qibla direction from wherever you stay.

Frequently asked questions

Is the mosque finder free?

Yes. It is free, runs in your browser and needs no app or account.

Why is a mosque missing from the map?

The map uses OpenStreetMap data, which volunteers maintain. Coverage varies by region, and anyone can add a missing mosque on openstreetmap.org.

Can I search for mosques in another city?

Yes. Type the city's name into the search box to move the map there.

Do I need to share my location?

No. Location shows mosques around you, but you can search for a city instead. Location is only used to calculate results and to look up the name of your city; no account is needed. See the QiblaFind privacy policy.
